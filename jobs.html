Position 1: Front-End Web Developer

Position Overview

Reference Number: DHW21

Job Title: Front-End Web Developer

Description: We are seeking a Front-End Web Developer to design and maintain responsive, accessible, and engaging interfaces for patient portals, appointment booking systems, and online health information pages.

Salary: $78,000 – $90,000 per year

Reports To: Digital Services Manager

Key Responsibilities

  1. Develop and maintain responsive website pages for patient-facing platforms.
  2. Work with content and design teams to improve usability and accessibility.
  3. Test interfaces across different browsers, devices, and screen sizes.
  4. Support updates to online appointment and information systems.
  5. Ensure website layouts remain visually consistent with company branding.

Requirements

Essential:

  • Strong knowledge of HTML5 and CSS3
  • Understanding of responsive web design principles
  • Ability to apply accessibility best practices
  • Good communication and teamwork skills
  • Strong attention to detail

Preferable:

  • Basic knowledge of JavaScript
  • Experience with Git or other version control tools
  • Previous exposure to healthcare or service-based websites

Position 2: Digital Content Coordinator

Position Overview

Reference Number: HLT5A

Job Title: Digital Content Coordinator

Description: We are looking for a Digital Content Coordinator to manage website content related to health services, patient support information, and digital service updates. The role focuses on clarity, accessibility, and accuracy.

Salary: $70,000 – $82,000 per year

Reports To: Online Communications Lead

Key Responsibilities

  1. Update and maintain health-related website content.
  2. Review information for accuracy, readability, and accessibility.
  3. Coordinate with internal teams to publish service announcements and updates.
  4. Support content planning for new digital health services.
  5. Ensure published information aligns with privacy and communication standards.

Requirements

Essential:

  • Strong written and verbal communication skills
  • Excellent organisational skills
  • Attention to detail and accuracy
  • Ability to manage multiple deadlines
  • Understanding of web content structure

Preferable:

  • Experience working with content management systems
  • Knowledge of accessibility principles
  • Interest in digital health communication
